## High Risk

### Dataset Quality

Poor labels can reduce model effectiveness.

Mitigation:
- Manual verification
- Consistent annotation guidelines

---

### Scope Creep

Too many features may delay delivery.

Mitigation:
- Follow roadmap strictly.

---

## Medium Risk

### Fusion Complexity

Combining modalities may introduce complexity.

Mitigation:
- Start simple
- Iterate gradually
